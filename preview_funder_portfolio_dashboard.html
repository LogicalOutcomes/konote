Why This Dashboard Is Possible

Without a common standard, each agency reports in its own format — different indicator names, different scales, different definitions of "success." A funder reviewing 87 programs would receive 87 incompatible spreadsheets.

With CIDS, each agency keeps its own terminology and measurement tools, but maps outcomes to shared codes at export time. The funder receives structured, comparable data — enabling the portfolio view above. The agency's workflow doesn't change; only the export gains a common language.

Data Integrity

All data is aggregate. No individual participant information is included in this dashboard. Each agency's data was approved by their designated report approver before submission. 24 of 26 received reports include evaluator attestation.
